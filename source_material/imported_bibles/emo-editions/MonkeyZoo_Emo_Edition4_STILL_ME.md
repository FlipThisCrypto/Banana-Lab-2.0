## Story Premise

A group of Emo Monkeys enters FusionZoo, a public facility that promises personal upgrades through a process called **fusion**.  
Fusion allows parts of different monkeys, traits, skills, or identities to be combined into something new.

The system is presented as safe, reversible, and empowering.  
As the group experiences the process, they confront the emotional cost of changing who they are in order to be more useful, valuable, or accepted.

---

## Characters

- **Moodz** — Protagonist  
  Emotionally expressive, guarded, skeptical, observant.

- **TwoTone** — Supporting  
  Balanced, analytical, emotionally split but controlled.

- **Static** — Supporting  
  Anxious, overstimulated, talks fast, reacts strongly.

- **Ash** — Supporting  
  Quiet, emotionally drained, speaks rarely.

- **NeonBlue** — Supporting  
  Optimistic on the surface, insecure underneath.

- **Scarline** — Observer  
  Grounded, experienced, speaks with clarity and restraint.

## Front Inside Cover

FusionZoo was built to help monkeys become more than they were.  
Not happier. Not calmer. Just… more.  
More capable. More efficient. More compatible.

They say you can always go back.  
They don’t talk much about what changes along the way.
