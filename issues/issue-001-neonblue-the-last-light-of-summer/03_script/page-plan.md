## What the extra 14 pages buy

The 8-page draft compressed the story to the point where several beats existed
only as captions. At 22 pages:

- **The festival becomes a place**, not a backdrop. Pages 1–4 establish
  geography, crowd, and what is at stake before anything breaks.
- **The team gets to be a team.** Page 2 gives all six distinct reactions to
  NeonBlue's volunteering, which the 8-page version had no room for.
- **The failure escalates.** Pages 5–7 let the blackout spread across three
  pages instead of one, so the pattern is felt before it is explained.
- **Lil Devil earns his discovery.** Page 12 gives him a full page: he is
  argued with, he does it anyway, and the consequence is his.
- **The splash lands.** Page 11 is a full-page reveal of the dark's shape —
  the page-turn from 10 to 11 is the issue's first big visual beat.
- **The wound has room.** Pages 15–17 give the Patch recognition three pages of
  quiet, including a two-panel page that is almost entirely silence.
- **The decision is a page turn.** Page 18 ends on the trapped three; page 19
  is two panels and nothing else.
- **The rescue is shown.** Pages 20–21 show the plan, the contributions, the
  strike and the exit, where the legacy version had captions.
