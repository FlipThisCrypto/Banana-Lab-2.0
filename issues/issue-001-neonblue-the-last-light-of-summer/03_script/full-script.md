# Issue 001 — The Last Light of Summer

**Full production script.** 8 pages, 27 panels (3/3/5/3/3/3/4/3).
Machine-readable form: `panel-script.yaml`. Where the two disagree, the YAML is
authoritative for production and this document is authoritative for intent.

Style standard: `canon/style/HOUSE_STYLE.md`. Panel art is **frameless and
textless** — borders, page ground, balloons, captions and SFX are added
downstream.

Panels are addressed `P<page>-<panel>`.

---

## PAGE 1 — Arrival
*Page frame: warm amber. Grid: one wide establishing over two smaller panels.*

### P1-01 — establishing, large
**Location** Festival grounds · **Shot** extreme wide, low angle · **Size** large

The last of the sun is behind the ferris wheel. Festival lights are just coming
on and losing the argument with daylight. The six Emo Monkeys enter frame from
the lower left, small against the grounds, walking in a loose group — not a
line. NeonBlue is a half-step ahead of everyone, already turned back toward
them, walking backwards, arms out.

> **CAPTION:** The last night of summer.

> **NEONBLUE:** Food stand needs hands. Lost-kid tent needs hands. We have
> twelve hands.

**Staging** Six characters, midground, on the main path. NeonBlue foremost and
largest; the other five fall back in depth with correct scale reduction. Long
shadows thrown toward camera by the low sun. Warm rim light on every silhouette.
**Purpose** Establish place, cast, and NeonBlue's habit of volunteering
everyone.

### P1-02 — reaction, small
**Location** Festival grounds · **Shot** medium two-shot · **Size** small

Lil Devil beside NeonBlue, grinning, entirely uninterested in the food stand.

> **LIL DEVIL:** Or we skip the queue and take what we want.

**Staging** Two figures, tight. Warm key from frame right. Lil Devil's red
reads hot against the amber.
**Purpose** Introduce the guest and his default setting. Comic, harmless — this
is the version of him that has not cost anything yet.

### P1-03 — the promise, medium
**Location** Festival main stage, distant · **Shot** wide · **Size** medium

The main stage across the grounds, countdown board glowing above it. NeonBlue
in the near foreground, half-turned, gesturing back at the stage like a man
presenting a gift.

> **NEONBLUE:** And at midnight the whole place lights up at once. You'll see.

**Staging** NeonBlue foreground left, in shadow against the bright stage.
Deliberate: the thing he is promising is the thing that will fail.
**Purpose** Plant the promise. Establish the stage as the place everyone will
be looking.

## PAGE 2 — The first failure
*Page frame: cooling magenta. Grid: two narrow verticals over one wide.*

### P2-01 — the tell, narrow vertical
**Location** Festival grounds, booth row · **Shot** medium close · **Size** small

Static, mid-step, stopped. Head tilted. Nobody around him has noticed anything.

> **STATIC:** Something just... stepped down.

**Staging** Static centred but small in a tall panel — the empty space above him
is the point. Booth lights still on and warm behind him.
**Purpose** Static perceives it first. He is right, and nobody is listening yet.

### P2-02 — the failure, narrow vertical
**Location** Festival grounds, booth row · **Shot** medium · **Size** small

The booth row behind NeonBlue drops dark, mid-sentence. He is still smiling,
still talking, one hand raised in reassurance. The backup panel beside him
shows nothing.

> **NEONBLUE:** — completely normal. Backup's already coming up, three, two —

> **SFX:** *tk*

**Staging** NeonBlue foreground right, lit only by what is left. The dark
travels left to right behind him. His raised hand is the last warm thing in
frame.
**Purpose** The promise fails while he is still making it. The reader sees the
gap before he does.

### P2-03 — the guest acts, wide
**Location** Festival grounds, service gate · **Shot** wide · **Size** medium

Lil Devil already at the locked service gate, hands on it. Scarline is a few
steps back, not moving toward him. NeonBlue is between them, caught.

> **SCARLINE:** Dark isn't the problem. What people do in it is.

> **LIL DEVIL:** Agreed. Let's do something.

**Staging** Three figures across the frame's width at different depths. Gate
practical light spills cyan-white onto Lil Devil's hands and face — the first
cyan in the issue, and it should not look important yet.
**Purpose** Scarline states the issue's real danger. Lil Devil proves her point
in the same breath.

## PAGE 3 — The pattern
*Page frame: cool magenta-cyan. Grid: dense — four small panels over one wide.*

### P3-01 — analysis, small
**Location** Festival grounds · **Shot** medium close · **Size** small

TwoTone at a festival map board, tracing two lines with two fingers.

> **TWOTONE:** Two failures. Different directions. Same speed.

**Staging** Close on the board and his hands. His face partly out of frame —
the information matters more than his reaction.
**Purpose** The failure is patterned. Established by observation, not exposition.

### P3-02 — the pulse, small
**Location** Festival grounds, projection screen · **Shot** insert · **Size** small

Hard insert on the public projection screen. The image has fractured into cyan
geometric shards. Static's reflection is visible in the glass, listening.

> **STATIC:** It's not noise. It's six things, over and over.

> **SFX:** *hmmm — hmmm — hmmm*

**Staging** Screen fills frame. Cyan light dominates. Static only as reflection.
**Purpose** Seed the six tones for Issue 002. The cyan fracture must be
visible — it was a caption in the legacy version.

### P3-03 — the crack begins, small
**Location** Festival grounds · **Shot** medium · **Size** small

NeonBlue herding people toward light, mid-laugh. Too wide. Too bright.

> **NEONBLUE:** This way! Best view's over here anyway!

**Staging** NeonBlue mid-frame, crowd silhouettes passing across the foreground
and partly occluding him. He is performing to a crowd that is not looking at him.
**Purpose** First visible over-brightness.

### P3-04 — Moodz watching, small
**Location** Festival grounds · **Shot** close · **Size** small

Moodz, still, watching NeonBlue work. He says nothing.

**Staging** Close on Moodz. Out-of-focus NeonBlue in the background, bright and
blurred. Silent panel.
**Purpose** Set up page 5. Moodz has already seen it.

### P3-05 — the grounds go, wide
**Location** Festival grounds · **Shot** extreme wide, high angle · **Size** large

High and wide over the grounds. A third of the festival is out now, and the
dark has a shape — two arms closing toward the middle.

> **CAPTION:** Two arms, closing.

**Staging** No characters, or characters too small to read. Pure information.
**Purpose** Give the reader the pattern from above before any character has it.
The page's anchor panel.

## PAGE 4 — The reaction
*Page frame: deep cyan. Grid: one large panel over two smalls.*

### P4-01 — the discovery, large
**Location** Festival control node · **Shot** wide, low angle · **Size** large

Lil Devil has forced the service gate. It hangs open. Behind him the control
box has responded — its amber status light has gone out and something under the
panel is glowing cyan through the seams. He is turned toward it, grin gone.
NeonBlue and Ash arrive at the frame edge.

> **LIL DEVIL:** I barely touched it.

> **SFX:** **FZZK-KRRR**

**Staging** Lil Devil midground, lit hard from below by the cyan seam light.
Cast shadow thrown up the wall behind him. NeonBlue and Ash foreground left,
partly occluded by the gate frame, in silhouette against the cyan.
**Purpose** **The discovery, and Lil Devil caused it.** The system reacts to
interference — not to NeonBlue. Ambiguous enough to be read as coincidence.
*(Conflict C-01 / C-02 — owner ruling pending.)*

### P4-02 — the denial, small
**Location** Festival control node · **Shot** medium close · **Size** small

NeonBlue looking at the glow, already explaining it away.

> **NEONBLUE:** That's a surge. That's what a surge looks like.

**Staging** Close. Cyan on one side of his face, nothing on the other. The
under-eye shadows read.
**Purpose** The last defence.

### P4-03 — Ash, small
**Location** Festival control node · **Shot** close two-shot · **Size** small

Ash, half-lidded, not looking at NeonBlue.

> **ASH:** Hope can read warnings.

**Staging** Two heads, tight, Ash foreground and slightly turned away. Cyan rim
on both.
**Purpose** Ash's one line in the issue, placed at the exact point of denial.

## PAGE 5 — The wound
*Page frame: darkest cyan-black. Grid: one tall vertical beside two stacked.*

### P5-01 — the marker, tall vertical
**Location** Festival service corridor · **Shot** extreme close, insert · **Size** large

Deep in the corridor. NeonBlue's hand on an old relay marker bolted to the
wall — a small plate, worn, with a frequency stamped into it. The stamp is
faintly cyan. His hand has stopped moving.

> **NEONBLUE:** ...Patch used this one.

**Staging** Tall panel. Marker and hand fill the upper two thirds. NeonBlue's
face enters only at the bottom edge, mostly dark. One practical light far down
the corridor.
**Purpose** The midpoint. The danger becomes personal through an object, not a
flashback. Patch is named once and never discussed.

### P5-02 — permission, medium
**Location** Festival service corridor · **Shot** medium two-shot · **Size** medium

Moodz has come down the corridor. He does not touch NeonBlue. He stands where
NeonBlue can see him.

> **MOODZ:** Your smile's wider than usual.

> **MOODZ:** You don't have to sell it to us.

**Staging** Two figures at slightly different depths, both on the corridor
floor with visible contact shadows, corridor practical behind them throwing rim
light on both. **This is the controlled-test panel.**
**Purpose** Moodz removes the obligation rather than issuing an instruction.
The quietest panel in the first half.

### P5-03 — the breath, medium
**Location** Festival service corridor · **Shot** wide · **Size** medium

NeonBlue alone in the corridor, facing away, one hand still on the wall. The
corridor runs off into dark.

**Staging** Silent. Figure small, low in frame, most of the panel empty
corridor. Deliberate negative space.
**Purpose** Let it land. The empty-panel technique from Edition Three.

## PAGE 6 — The choice
*Page frame: near-black with one red practical. Grid: two wides over one large.*

### P6-01 — the pull, wide
**Location** Festival main stage · **Shot** extreme wide, low angle from crowd · **Size** medium

The crowd massed at the main stage, seen from within it and below. The
countdown board is lit and everything else is not. Every face is pointed the
same way.

> **SFX:** *TEN — NINE —*

**Staging** Crowd silhouettes filling the lower frame, backlit hard by the
stage. No cast member identifiable. The whole festival is looking one way.
**Purpose** Establish what NeonBlue is being pulled toward.

### P6-02 — the other thing, wide
**Location** Festival service corridor · **Shot** wide, low angle · **Size** medium

A jammed emergency shutter, half down. Behind the grating, three young
festival-goers — small, distinct, frightened, not hurt. Static has his hand flat
on the metal. Scarline is beside him.

> **STATIC:** Three. Behind the shutter.

**Staging** The three trapped figures must be **clearly visible and individually
distinct** through the grating. Static and Scarline foreground, partly
silhouetted. The single red corridor practical is the only strong light.
**Purpose** The stakes get faces. Established here so page 7 has someone in it.

### P6-03 — the decision, large
**Location** Between corridor and grounds · **Shot** medium, slightly low · **Size** large

NeonBlue at the junction. The countdown light comes from one side, the red
corridor light from the other. He is lit by both. Nobody is telling him
anything. Scarline is behind him at the frame edge, not reaching for him.

> **SCARLINE:** Both of those are real choices.

**Staging** NeonBlue centred but not symmetrical — pushed toward the corridor
side of the frame before he has moved. Two light sources of different colours
on either side of his face. Scarline small, background, deliberately not
intervening.
**Purpose** **The decision.** Scarline validates without deciding. The page's
anchor panel and the emotional centre of the issue.

## PAGE 7 — The work
*Page frame: warming amber returning. Grid: irregular five-panel.*

### P7-01 — the honest plan, medium
**Location** Festival service corridor · **Shot** medium wide · **Size** medium

NeonBlue with the team around him. He is not smiling. He is pointing at the
shutter hinge.

> **NEONBLUE:** I can't promise the grid holds.

> **NEONBLUE:** Here's how we get them out anyway.

**Staging** Five characters in a **worked arrangement** — different depths,
different heights, some crouched, some turned away, all oriented toward the
hinge he is indicating. Not a row. Torch light from below.
**Purpose** Hope that admits risk. The blocking must read as a working team, not
a group portrait.

### P7-02 — the contributions, two smalls
**Location** Festival service corridor · **Shot** close inserts · **Size** small ×2

Two tight panels side by side. TwoTone's hand on a route diagram. Static's face,
counting.

> **TWOTONE:** Left conduit's still live. Go under it.

> **STATIC:** Surge every nine seconds. Go on eight.

**Staging** Two inserts, tight, no full figures.
**Purpose** Static and TwoTone act at the climax, which the legacy version
dropped.

### P7-03 — the strike, medium
**Location** Festival service corridor · **Shot** medium, low angle · **Size** medium

Lil Devil hits the hinge exactly where NeonBlue marked it. Sparks. The shutter
gives.

> **LIL DEVIL:** Precision. Fine. I can do precision.

> **SFX:** **KRAK!**

**Staging** Dynamic. Impact burst at the hinge, sparks thrown across the frame,
debris. Lil Devil mid-strike with real weight through the swing. Full house-style
SFX treatment — angled, overlapping the panel border.
**Purpose** Directed force works. His growth is visible, not stated.

### P7-04 — the payoff, large
**Location** Festival service corridor · **Shot** wide · **Size** large

The shutter is up. The three come out. NeonBlue is not in the middle of the
frame — he is at the side, hand out, counting them past him.

> **NEONBLUE:** That's three. That's everyone.

**Staging** The three rescued figures are the subject and must be clearly
visible. NeonBlue frame right, secondary. Warm light spilling in from the
grounds behind them. Contact shadows on all five figures.
**Purpose** The rescue happens on the page. NeonBlue is not centred, because
this was never about him being seen.

## PAGE 8 — After
*Page frame: warm amber and cyan together. Grid: one wide, one large, one insert.*

### P8-01 — reduced, wide
**Location** Festival grounds · **Shot** wide · **Size** medium

The grounds, half lit. Some rides are dark. The crowd is smaller and calmer.
NeonBlue sits on the edge of something with Moodz beside him. Lil Devil is
nearby, unusually still.

> **NEONBLUE:** Stage finale was half-lit. Carousel never came back.

> **NEONBLUE:** Everyone walked out. That's a good night.

**Staging** Must be visibly **a different view of the grounds than page 1**, and
visibly half-lit. Warm restored light on the left, dead dark on the right.
**Purpose** He does not oversell it. The best line in the issue.

### P8-02 — the Echo, insert
**Location** Festival grounds, junction tower · **Shot** extreme close, insert · **Size** small

Hard insert on a dark panel high on a junction tower. One segment of a larger
shape has come alive in cyan. The rest of the shape is dark and incomplete and
the panel does not show its edges.

> **SFX:** *tk*

**Staging** Silent. No characters. The lit segment is small in frame, partial
and off-centre. **Exactly one segment. The full symbol is never shown.**
**Purpose** The Echo answers the choice, after the choice.
*(Conflict C-01 — owner ruling pending.)*

### P8-03 — the question, large
**Location** Festival grounds · **Shot** medium wide, low angle · **Size** large

NeonBlue from behind and below, looking up at the tower — at the wrong part of
it. He cannot see the lit segment. Ash stands nearby, also looking up, and it is
not clear whether he can.

> **NEONBLUE:** It went cyan when I got close. Why would it know me?

> **CAPTION:** The question stays.

**Staging** Both figures small, low in frame, tower and night sky above. The lit
segment is visible to the reader and not to them. Warm ground light, cold sky.
**Purpose** Close the issue, open the season. The reader knows something the
character does not.

## Script notes

**Balloon discipline** No balloon exceeds 15 words. No panel carries more than
two balloons. NeonBlue's longest single balloon is 11 words.

**Silence** Five panels have no dialogue: P3-04, P5-03, P8-02, and the
non-speaking beats in P3-05 and P6-01 carry only caption or SFX. The published
editions use silence freely and the legacy version used it never.

**Removed from the legacy script** "three main zones, eight rides", "five
thousand people", "a thirteen-year-old system". All invented specifics that
constrained later issues.

**Speaking count** NeonBlue 9, Lil Devil 3, Static 3, TwoTone 2, Scarline 2,
Moodz 2, Ash 1. Matches the season bible's supporting distribution.
